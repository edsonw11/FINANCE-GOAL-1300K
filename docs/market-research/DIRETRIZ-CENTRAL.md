# Central directive of the product (approved by the owner, 2026-11-27)

> The user does not need to know how to organise their finances. They only use their money normally. The app takes care of the organisation, shows what is happening, and asks for help only when needed.

## Honest boundary

The app works when it **receives data**; it does not know everything. Open Finance is out of the product (owner decision). The entry channels still to be validated are: files the user brings (OFX, CSV, PDF), Android notifications, and SMS or e-mail (no evidence yet that it works). Until a channel is measured with real extracts, nothing is promised as "automatic".

## Rules for the strategy phase

- No screens, refactors or features until the next three deliveries are approved: (1) channel test with the owner's real extracts, (2) complementary research (Reddit, Google Play, hands-on with 2 or 3 competitors), (3) ideal experience on paper, comparing today, competitors and target.
- Automation never saves what it is unsure about without the user's confirmation, and what the app resolved must be visible and reversible.
