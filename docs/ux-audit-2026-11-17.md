## A. Executive diagnosis

**What is good (keep):**
- Numbers are verifiable: almost every KPI opens the list of records that add up to it (`summary-details.js`, `month-summary.js`), and a calculation audit test exists. Few personal finance apps do this.
- Safety: nothing is saved without confirmation in imports; rollback on failed save (`store.transact`); automatic daily copy; recovery of unreadable storage; destructive actions behind confirm or "⋯".
- Brazilian formats everywhere (R$ mask, dd/mm/aaaa, card cycle with "melhor dia de compra").
- Fast entry options: quick text entry ("gastei 85,90 no mercado hoje"), category chips from history, repeat a record, import CSV/OFX/PDF with review.
- Mobile basics are solid: bottom bar, FAB, sheets, 44 px targets, no horizontal scroll.

**Biggest problems:**
1. **The Summary contradicts itself.** Same month, test data: "Taxa de poupança 35,0% · Excelente" next to "Sobrou no caixa −R$ 2.489,79" in red. Both are correct under different rules (savings rate ignores contributions and card timing; "Sobrou no caixa" subtracts contributions and paid invoices). A user cannot tell which one is "how much is left". In a finance app, two answers to the same question destroy trust.
2. **The Summary is too long and its most useful block is last.** 13 blocks; on a phone it is more than 3.600 px (5+ screens). "Onde gastei no mês" (spending by group, the question users ask most) is the **last** card, after two analysis sections. "Média mensal R$ 4.396" appears twice (in "Gastos mês a mês" and in "Análise dos gastos").
3. **"How much do I have today?" has no answer.** The app has no bank account balance: all bank accounts are one implicit "Conta" and only investments have balances. Organizze leads with "Saldo de todos os bancos numa tela". Here the closest numbers are "Sobrou no caixa" (a month flow, not a balance) and "Patrimônio líquido" (investments only).
4. **Search only works inside the selected month** (`filterTransactions(month, f)`), and it is accent-sensitive ("credito" does not find "Crédito"). "Find an old record" is a basic task that fails.
5. **Two different "open invoice" numbers.** Summary KPI "Fatura em aberto R$ 5.766,00" (every unpaid card purchase up to month end) vs Card tab "Fatura atual R$ 541,00". Same words, different meaning.
6. **Card tab month arrows do almost nothing.** The big invoice card always shows today's invoice (`renderCycles` uses `today()`); the arrows only change the bottom "faturas pagas neste mês" card. The control looks broken.

**Risks:** trust (problems 1, 5), abandonment of the Summary (2), and silent data mistakes: typing a category name that does not exist **creates a new category without asking** (`transaction-builder.js` → `ensureCat`). A typo ("Mercdo") becomes a category and splits the spending.

Overall: the engine (rules, import, verifiability) is above market level for a personal app; the **presentation layer has grown by accumulation** and now needs subtraction more than addition.

## C. Audit by screen

Format: **where** — what the user feels → why it is bad → consequence → fix → gain.

### C1. First visit (empty app)
- **Backup banner on an empty app** — "Você ainda não fez backup" before there is anything to back up → noise that sounds like a warning → the first message the product gives is a scolding → show it only after N records or 7 days → calmer, credible first contact.
- **No starting point** — six zero cards and "—"; nothing says "start here" → user does not know the first step → many leave → an empty-state card on top of Resumo: 3 steps ("Lance sua primeira despesa", "Importe um extrato", "Defina sua meta") with buttons → time to first value drops from minutes to seconds.
- **Brand "Meta R$ 1,3 M"** — reads as a number, not a name; a new user does not know what the app is → name + a one-line purpose ("Controle do mês e da sua meta"); show the goal in the Patrimônio tab, not as the brand.

### C2. Resumo
- **Contradicting "what is left"** (see A.1) → pick **one** month result and name it plainly. Recommendation: the hero shows "Sobrou no mês = receitas − despesas" (what the savings rate uses) and, below it, "Desse valor, você investiu R$ X" and "Ficou na conta R$ Y". "Sobrou no caixa" (cash timing with invoices) moves into the detail sheet. One question, one number, explained.
- **Length and order** → reorder by the questions in section 6 and cut duplicates: (1) what to do now, (2) month result hero, (3) Receitas / Despesas / Investido, (4) Onde gastei no mês, (5) Orçamento (only if defined) and Contas do mês (collapsed to "9 de 9 pagas ✅" when all paid), (6) Gastos mês a mês. Move "Análise dos gastos" to its own screen ("Análise") reachable from the chart. Remove "Receitas × despesas × aportes (6 meses)" (the trend chart already answers "how am I vs last months").
- **"Contas do mês" list of 9 paid items** — long and done → when all are paid, show one line; expand on tap.
- **"✕" on each bill** means "não é conta fixa" but looks like delete or close → replace by "⋯ → Não é conta fixa".
- **Fatura em aberto R$ 5.766** vs Cartão R$ 541 → rename the KPI "Cartão: a pagar (todas as faturas)" and show "fatura atual R$ 541" as the main number, with older unpaid invoices as a warning.
- **"Leitura dos dados" (6 insight boxes)** — useful content but 6 at once is a wall → keep the 2 most relevant in the Summary, the rest in Análise.
- **"Aportes (investido)" "→ R$ 5.456,83" in blue with an arrow** — the arrow is a third sign beside + and −; acceptable, but the label changes across screens ("Aporte (investir)", "Aportes (investido)", "Aporte") → see section 8.

### C3. Lançamentos
- **Four ways to add on the same screen** (FAB, 4 type buttons, inline form, quick entry) on desktop → choice overload → desktop: keep quick entry + one "Novo lançamento" form; phone: keep FAB + quick entry, drop the 4 big buttons (the sheet already has the type tabs).
- **Search limited to the month** (A.4) → when the search box has text, search **all months** and group results by month; accent-insensitive (reuse `normalizeText`).
- **"Excluir" in red on every row (desktop)** — the Card tab already moved delete behind the edit dialog; here it is one click from "Editar" on 17 rows → move to the detail / "⋯" and add **undo** in the toast ("Lançamento excluído · Desfazer") instead of only a confirm.
- **"Saldo da lista: − R$ 3.030,79"** — "saldo" suggests a bank balance; it is income minus everything else including contributions → rename "Resultado da lista" or remove; show the totals per type instead.
- **Category free text creates categories silently** → when the typed name is new, show "Criar categoria ‘Mercdo’?" inline, with the closest existing match suggested ("Você quis dizer Mercado?").
- **"Pago com" select truncated on phone** ("Conta / Pix / dé") → shorter labels ("Conta", "Cartão", "VR/VA").
- **Footer formula on this tab** — "Projeção: saldoₙ = …" is irrelevant here → show it only in Patrimônio.

### C4. Cartão
- **Month arrows do not move the main invoice** (A.6) → the arrows should select the invoice (previous / next), or be removed from this tab and replaced by "‹ fatura anterior · próxima ›" on the invoice card.
- **Phone: each purchase is a 4-line card with labels** (Data/Categoria/Descrição/Valor + Editar) → two purchases fill a screen; use the same row as Lançamentos (icon, title, date · category, value).
- **Bug — total row on phone:** the stacked table labels the total row as "Data: Total da fatura · Categoria: R$ 541,00 · Descrição:" (`stack-table` applies header labels to the footer row) → render the total as a plain line.
- **Only one card** (`CARD()` is a single setting) → common Brazilian case is 2–3 cards; see backlog P2 (only if the user really has more than one; not urgent for a single user).
- "Nenhuma fatura paga neste mês" as a full card → fold into one line under the invoice.

### C5. Patrimônio
- **Two charts for the same question** ("will I reach the goal?"): tower chart and "Projeção mês a mês" line chart. On both, the goal line (R$ 1,3 M) squeezes real data into the bottom 10% of the chart → keep **one** chart: real net worth of the last 12–24 months with a short projection and the goal as a label ("Meta: R$ 1,3 M em ~2031"), y-axis scaled to the data, not to the goal.
- **13-row projection table** → collapse under "Ver mês a mês".
- **"Sem saldo informado" on Ações/FIIs/ETFs while showing R$ 10.441,21** → the value is the cost of the portfolio; say "custo da carteira (atualize o saldo)" to avoid "is this number real?".
- **Each account row has "🛟 Reserva" + "arquivar"** — on phone they stack and each row becomes ~150 px; "arquivar" is lowercase, others capitalised → move both into "⋯" per account; show a 🛟 badge on the reserve account.
- **Bug — donut labels clipped on phone:** "Renda fix", "eserva de emergênc…", "Tesouro Selic (te…" at 390 px → use the legend-list layout under the ring on narrow screens (name, %, value), keep callouts only on wide screens.
- **Section order** → today: hero, 2 charts, table, then investments. Put "Onde está meu dinheiro" and "Distribuição" **before** the projection: users open this tab to see balances; the projection is a secondary view.

### C6. Categorias
- **Budget field on every group** (8 "sem limite" inputs) → budgets are optional; show "Definir limite" as a link, the input only after tapping.
- Income categories with large switches for 0-use categories → acceptable; consider hiding unused default categories behind "mostrar categorias sem uso".

### C7. Ajustes
- **"Premissas", "Meta líquida", "Rendimento líquido (% ao ano)"** are finance jargon → "Sua meta", "Quanto seus investimentos rendem por ano, já sem IR (%)".
- **Personal text in the UI:** "(seu relatório usa 11,16%)" — refers to a document the app does not know → remove or make it a generic hint.
- Card settings live under "Premissas" (goal assumptions) → move to a "Cartão" block (or into the Card tab, "⚙ ajustar datas").

### C8. Sheets (form, detail, import)
- Good: title + subtitle, close button, Esc/back closes (`sheet.js`), sticky actions.
- Import sheet: honest format chips ("Foto, PDF escaneado e Excel: ainda não") — keep.
- Detail sheets list records with the same total as the card — keep; this is the product's trust differentiator.

## F. Forms

**Fastest path today for a common expense (phone):** FAB → type value → tap a category chip → "Adicionar despesa" = **4 taps + typing the value**. Date defaults to today, "Pago com" to Conta. This is already good (on par with market apps). Quick entry is 1 field + "Preencher" + confirm.

Problems and fixes:
- Category is a free-text input with a datalist: new names are created silently (C3). Fix: confirm new names, suggest the closest.
- "Pago com" default is always "Conta"; the user who mostly uses the card must change it each time → default to the **last used** source (per type).
- The value field is first and focused — good. Enter on the value field should not submit an incomplete form; the save button is disabled while saving (`transaction-save.js`) — good (double-tap protected).
- "Pagar fatura" as a type tab next to Despesa/Receita confuses beginners ("is paying the invoice an expense?"). Keep it (it is needed for the cash flow) but explain in one line under the tab: "Não é gasto novo: as compras já contaram".
- Repeat / split under "Mais opções" — done; keep.
- Validation messages are inline next to the field (`field-error.js`) — good.
- Investment account form (Patrimônio) is a single name field — good. Balance update form ("Atualizar saldos") is a grid of net/gross per account — fine on desktop, long on phone; consider "update one account" from its row.
- Missing: **edit history / undo** for delete and for bulk actions (import, "Apagar tudo" has confirm only).

Do not implement automatic saving of suggestions without review (current behaviour is correct: suggestions only pre-fill).

## G. Design system and iconography

The app already has tokens (`01-tokens.css`), a contrast test, a no-inline-style rule and 44 px targets. Gaps:

- **Icons:** two systems mixed — line SVG icons in nav/KPIs (`icons.js`) and emoji in categories, section headings and buttons (🏦 💳 ⚡ 📥 🛟 🔁). Emoji render differently per OS and some carry meaning alone (🔴 🟡 in "O que fazer agora"). Rule: SVG for UI actions and navigation; emoji only for user-chosen category icons; status always with text + colour (already mostly true).
- **Same function, different icons/labels:** contribution = 🏦 / "→" / "Aporte (investir)" / "Aportes (investido)"; remove = "Excluir" (button), "✕" (bills), "×" (names), "arquivar" (accounts), "Tirar da importação". Standardise: "Excluir" for data records (inside detail/⋯), "Remover" for list membership (names, bills), "Arquivar" capitalised.
- **Buttons:** primary (filled blue), secondary (outlined), danger (red text). Danger buttons appear inline in lists (Lançamentos desktop) → rule: danger actions only inside a detail, dialog or ⋯.
- **Cards:** some cards are tappable (› chevron), others look the same and are not (Taxa de poupança, Reserva) → rule: chevron = tappable; non-tappable info cards have no chevron and a lighter style.
- **Headings:** section headings in two styles (uppercase small "NOVO LANÇAMENTO", "PREMISSAS" vs bold card titles) → keep uppercase only for page sections, card titles in sentence case.
- **Colours:** green = income/good, red = expense/bad, blue = investment, amber = attention; consistent. One issue: "Sobrou no caixa" uses red for a negative cash timing that is not "bad" (money was invested) → solved by E.2.
- **States:** empty states exist and are explanatory; loading exists only in imports (spinner/phases); no skeletons needed (local data is instant).

## Section 8 — proposed glossary

| Own transfer | Transferência entre suas contas | — |
| Investments total | Patrimônio | Patrimônio líquido (only in the detail) |
| Remove from a list | Remover | ✕ alone |
| Delete a record | Excluir | — |
